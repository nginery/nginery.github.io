
Privacy Policy — Nova Bricks
Last Updated: November 15, 2026

1. General
Nova Bricks (the “App”) is a brick-breaker arcade game published under the package name com.novabricks.game. This Privacy Policy explains how the App processes data.

2. Data We Collect
Local game progress, settings, and preferences: Unlocked chapters, stars, best score, saved games, language, and sound settings are stored only on your device. The App has no user accounts and does not send this data to our servers.
Advertising ID and device- or advertising-related technical data: Processed by Google AdMob to display advertisements: full-screen ads between chapters, and optional rewarded ads (for example, to continue after a game over).
Usage analytics: Information such as level starts, level results, chapter completions, and app sessions, together with basic device information (such as device model, operating system version, and app version) and an app instance identifier, collected through Google Analytics for Firebase. Analytics does not collect the advertising ID, and this data is not used for advertising.
Crash and error reports: When the App crashes or hits an error, a report with technical details (such as the error message and stack trace, the level being played, device model, operating system version, app version, and an installation identifier) is collected through Firebase Crashlytics.
Consent choices: Your privacy and ad consent choices are collected and stored through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where required by law (for example, in the EEA, the UK, and certain US states).
The App does not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, or other personal information.

3. How We Use Data
We use data to save your game progress and settings on your device, understand how the game is played so we can improve levels and difficulty, find and fix crashes and errors, show advertisements, and ask for and respect your consent choices for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

5. Data Retention
Local game data remains on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the App, or by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. If Android backup is turned on, your device may include this data in your personal Google backup, which is controlled by your device settings.

Analytics, crash report, advertising, and consent data are retained in accordance with Google's data retention policies and the retention settings of our Google Analytics property.

6. Children’s Privacy
The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your Rights and Choices
You can review or change your ad consent choices at any time with Settings → Privacy & ad settings in the App, where available in your region.
You can reset or opt out of ad personalization through your device settings (for example, Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android).
You can delete your local game progress with Settings → Reset progress.
The App does not require user account creation.

8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when the updated policy is posted, and the “Last Updated” date above will change.
