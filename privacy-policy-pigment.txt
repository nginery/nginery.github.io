# Privacy Policy — Pigment

**Last Updated: October 30, 2026**

## 1. General

Pigment (the “App”) is a color-based puzzle game published under the package name `com.pigment.app`. This Privacy Policy explains how the App processes data.

## 2. Data We Collect

- **Local game progress, settings, and preferences:** Stored on your device and not sent to our servers through a user account.
- **Room and multiplayer game data:** Information required to create and join multiplayer rooms, participate in games, and synchronize gameplay between players.
- **Usage analytics:** Information such as game sessions, level starts, game completions, and screen views, collected through Firebase Analytics.
- **Crash and error reports:** Collected through Firebase Crashlytics.
- **Advertising ID and device- or advertising-related technical data:** Processed by Google AdMob to display advertisements.

## 3. How We Use Data

We use data to provide multiplayer gameplay functionality, synchronize game sessions, improve analytics and app stability, display advertisements, and enhance the overall user experience.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

- **Firebase Analytics**
- **Firebase Crashlytics**
- **Firebase Firestore**
- **Google AdMob**

These services are subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google AdMob Privacy Information: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
- Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 5. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device and can be reset through the App's Settings menu, where available.

Multiplayer room data is stored and processed as necessary to provide multiplayer functionality and is retained according to the applicable data management practices.

Analytics, crash reporting, and advertising data are retained in accordance with the applicable data retention policies of the relevant third-party services.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You can limit ad personalization through your device settings and manage your local game progress within the App.

The App does not require user account creation. However, certain multiplayer features may process temporary player or room-related identifiers to support gameplay.
